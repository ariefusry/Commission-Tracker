## LinkedIn

if you take art commissions and you're still juggling spreadsheets, notes apps, or just... memory? i made a free web app that honestly just makes life easier.

you get a drag-and-drop board where you move commissions from waiting list to in progress to done. it auto-calculates prices from your order types and add-ons, tracks who paid, who hasn't, how much they still owe. basically all the annoying admin stuff you don't wanna think about.

and the part i think is genuinely cool is that every commission gets a private tracking link you can send to your client. they can check their own progress, see where they are in your queue, even get an estimated finish date. no more getting DMs asking "is it done yet?" every 3 days.

you can also upload WIPs so clients can follow along, set up deadline reminders through Discord, and there's an income dashboard so you actually know how much you're making. works on phone too and you can install it like an app. oh and dark mode obviously.

it's completely free. no trial, no "pay to unlock features", nothing. there's a donation option if you feel like it but genuinely no pressure.

worth checking out if you do commissions

## LinkedIn Project Section

**Project name**
Commission Tracker

**Description**
A free web app for illustrators who take art commissions. Tracks every order from waiting list to delivery with a drag-and-drop board, auto-calculates prices from order types and add-ons, and shows who paid, who still owes, and how much.

Each commission gets a private tracking link for the client so they can check their own progress, queue position, and estimated finish date without needing to ask.

Features include WIP image uploads (browser-compressed, auto-deleted after 30 days), daily deadline reminders via Discord, an income and analytics dashboard, manual priority ordering, slot limits with automatic queuing, bilingual support (Indonesian and English), dark mode, and PWA install.

Security is enforced at the database level with row-level security on every table. The public tracking page reads through a dedicated Postgres function that returns only safe, non-sensitive data.

Built as a solo full-stack project from problem research to production deployment.

**Project link**
https://commission-tracker-one.vercel.app
